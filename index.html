@freekatet // critical design

Currently in NYC

FREEKA TET is a French-born, New York City-based digital artist, director and creative mercenary working at the edges of experimental art, music and stage.
His practice combines objects, prosthetics, animatronics, puppetry, hacking, code, electronics, audio & video plunderphonics, bricolage and performance.
Drawing on internet culture, memes, trolls and irrational human behaviour, Tet approaches every medium with a hacker's mindset: bending tools away from their intended use and blurring creative expression with technical execution.

He currently designs the show and performs live visuals for Oneohtrix Point Never's "Tranquilizer" tour, after touring the "Again" show as a live puppeteer, manipulating and filming miniature sets and figures in real time. He also designs VTSS's live show, from the Coachella 2025 A/V set to the 2026 tour, and directed The Weeknd ft. Anitta's "São Paulo" video as well as OPN's "A Barely Lit Path" and "Memories of Music".
With Amnesia Scanner he released the double album "HOAX" (2024). His half, FT HOAX, is his debut full-length: the AS record negated and erased through custom active noise cancelling scripts. It follows their collaborative album and live A/V show STROBE.RIP on PAN.

His work has been shown at Google I/O, MUTEK, Fondation Cartier, Art Basel, Coachella, the Montreal Biennale and more.
Over the years he has conceived stages, directed videos and consulted for The Weeknd, Ye, Oneohtrix Point Never, Aphex Twin, Childish Gambino, Amnesia Scanner, VTSS, Machine Gun Kelly, SebastiAn, Battles, Chromeo, Duck Sauce, Gesaffelstein and Jackson & His Computer Band, for clients including Margiela, Collina Strada, Google, Adidas, Prada, YSL, Nike, H&M, W Hotels and Levi’s.
Every facet of the work keeps mutating, moving between modes of visual and sound generation on reflex: intuitive and playful.

Music released on: PAN, Warp, Ipecac, Le Dernier Cri, VVM Vukzid, School of Meat Cutting, Fathme, Cock Rock Disco, Hirntrust, Wwilko, Tozitodik, Grindcore Karaoke.
